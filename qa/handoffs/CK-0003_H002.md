## Expected Behavior
- `python scripts/cadkit_qa_bridge.py ortho <root> off` should disable Ortho without any
  OS-level key injection or direct edits to `~/.config/cadkit/prefs.json`.
- `python scripts/cadkit_qa_bridge.py ortho <root> on` should enable Ortho through the
  same shared app logic as the live `F8` path.
- `python scripts/cadkit_qa_bridge.py ortho <root>` should toggle Ortho.
- `state.json` should immediately reflect the new `ortho_enabled` state after the action.
- The `CK-0003` snap scenarios should now be repeatable without touching Bill's real
  preferences file.

## Required Test Scenarios
1. Verify `ortho off` flips `state.json` to `ortho_enabled: false`.
2. Verify `ortho on` flips `state.json` to `ortho_enabled: true`.
3. Verify bare `ortho` toggles the current state.
4. Re-run the `CK-0003` perpendicular, tangent, and nearest setups without editing
   `~/.config/cadkit/prefs.json`, confirming the bridge action is sufficient.
5. Confirm the `CK-0003_R001` preference-file workaround is no longer needed.
6. Clean shutdown through the bridge `quit` path.
